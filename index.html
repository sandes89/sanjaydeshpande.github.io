01 / Profile

About

Scientist at the Indian Biological Data Centre, Regional Centre for Biotechnology.

Dr. Deshpande leads IBDC nucleotide data portals and works on the curation, quality control, annotation and archiving of biological data in India's national life-science repository, following FAIR principles and metadata standards.

His experience includes management of the GenomeIndia database (GI-DB) and SARS-CoV-2 genomic surveillance data, alongside RNA-seq, differential expression, comparative transcriptomics, microbial genome analysis and genome mining.
